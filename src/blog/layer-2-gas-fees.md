# Layer 2 Gas Fees: How Arbitrum, Optimism, and Base Reduce Costs

As Ethereum's popularity continues to grow, so do its gas fees. During periods of high network congestion, simple transactions can cost several dollars or more. This has led to the development of Layer 2 scaling solutions that significantly reduce gas costs while maintaining the security of the Ethereum mainnet.

In this article, we'll explore how popular Layer 2 solutions achieve lower gas fees and compare their costs using our [GasNow tracker](https://gasnow.link/).

## What Are Layer 2 Solutions?

Layer 2 solutions are protocols built on top of Ethereum (Layer 1) that handle transactions off-chain before settling the final state back to the mainnet. This approach dramatically reduces the computational load on Ethereum, resulting in significantly lower gas fees.

## How Layer 2 Solutions Reduce Gas Fees

Layer 2 solutions reduce gas fees through several mechanisms:

1. **Off-chain Computation**: Most transaction processing happens off-chain, reducing the load on Ethereum mainnet.
2. **Batching**: Multiple transactions are bundled together and submitted to Ethereum as a single transaction.
3. **Data Compression**: Transaction data is compressed before being posted to Ethereum, reducing costs.
